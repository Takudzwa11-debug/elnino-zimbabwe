# El Niño and Zimbabwe's rains: summary of findings

Seasons analysed: 1981/82 to 2025/26, 10 provincial towns.

## Rainfall by ENSO phase

| Phase | Town-seasons | Mean rainfall vs normal | Share below normal (driest third) |
|---|---|---|---|
| La Nina | 160 | +12.7% | 10% |
| Neutral | 140 | -1.3% | 34% |
| El Nino | 100 | -14.6% | 53% |
| Strong El Nino | 50 | -31.3% | 94% |

* El Niño seasons were drier than neutral seasons (one-sided Mann-Whitney U test, p = 7.71e-12).
* In strong El Niño seasons, 94% of town-seasons fell in the driest third, against 33% expected by chance.

## Driest seasons on record (national average)

* 1991/92: -41% (ENSO phase: Strong El Nino)
* 2023/24: -35% (ENSO phase: Strong El Nino)
* 1981/82: -33% (ENSO phase: Neutral)
* 1982/83: -31% (ENSO phase: Strong El Nino)
* 2001/02: -30% (ENSO phase: Neutral)

## Most El Niño-sensitive towns (correlation with ONI)

* Marondera (Mashonaland East): r = -0.71 whole season, -0.51 Jan–Mar
* Harare: r = -0.66 whole season, -0.45 Jan–Mar
* Mutare (Manicaland): r = -0.66 whole season, -0.51 Jan–Mar
* Chinhoyi (Mashonaland West): r = -0.66 whole season, -0.54 Jan–Mar

## Harvests

* Cereal yield vs ONI: r = -0.30 (p = 0.0511, n = 43). Each +1°C on the ONI goes with about -10% yield against the previous five harvests.
